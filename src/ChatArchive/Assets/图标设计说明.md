用户最终选择恢复第一版青绿色，移除所有明暗变化和立体效果。当前实际使用 `chat-archive.svg` 矢量源，底色为均匀 #0A9B7D，图形为均匀 #FFFFFF，外部透明；没有渐变、阴影、高光或纹理。通过 `render-icon.ps1` 原生WPF渲染为PNG，再封装多尺寸ICO。已核对背景多个位置像素一致。

按用户提供的绿色图标参考，使用内置 image_gen 编辑为更明亮、接近微信的绿色，主色目标 #07C160。旧版保留为 chat-archive-emerald-v1.png / .ico，当前程序仍引用 chat-archive.png / .ico。

本次最终编辑提示词：

Edit this existing Windows application icon. Change ONLY its green color treatment: replace the dark teal/emerald with a vivid fresh WeChat-like spring green, dominant #07C160 with subtle brighter #12D76B highlights and modest #05AA52 shadows. The result should look bright green rather than blue-green or dark forest green, visually close to the green app icon in the user's screenshot. Preserve exactly the centered rounded-square tile, white chat bubble with three holes, export arrow and tray, their positions, shapes, proportions, and ample padding. Preserve actual alpha transparency outside the tile. Keep shading much gentler and brighter than the original, with white glyph crisp and legible at small sizes. No text, no extra logos, no notification badge. Deliver the single revised icon.
